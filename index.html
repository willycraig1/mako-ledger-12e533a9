    Future development

    Splitting gas by who ran the boat

    Right now, fuel is split like any other shared cost. A later version could split each fill-up by who used the boat.

    • Log each outing: date, which family, and engine hours (the Aug service read 23.4 hrs) or gallons.
    • Assign each fill-up, marina gas or a dock pump, to the outings it covered.
    • Choose a rule per fill-up: by trips, by engine hours, or a flat 50/50.
    • Fold the result into the split above, so the balance shows who owes whom.
